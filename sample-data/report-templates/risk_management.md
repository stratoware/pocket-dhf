### Risk Management Methodology

This risk management process follows ISO 14971:2019 and includes:

1. **Risk Identification** - Systematic identification of hazards and hazardous situations
2. **Risk Analysis** - Estimation of probability, severity, and risk levels
3. **Risk Evaluation** - Assessment of acceptability and comparison against criteria
4. **Risk Control** - Implementation of measures to reduce risks
5. **Residual Risk Evaluation** - Assessment of remaining risk after controls
6. **Risk-Benefit Analysis** - Evaluation of clinical benefits vs. residual risks
7. **Risk Management Review** - Periodic review and monitoring
8. **Post-Market Surveillance** - Ongoing monitoring of risk effectiveness

### Risk Acceptability Criteria

Risks are evaluated using ISO 14971:2019 risk scoring methodology (Severity × Probability of Harm):

| Risk Score Range | Acceptability | Action Required |
|------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **0-2** | Acceptable | Monitor and document |
| **3-6** | Acceptable with Justification | Implement controls if feasible |
| **> 6** | Unacceptable | Implement additional controls immediately |
